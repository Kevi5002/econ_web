How It Works

Editorial Process

  • Submit

    Send an abstract and draft through the submission form below, any time during the quarter.

  • Peer review

    Two student editors review for argument, data, and clarity, and return notes within two weeks.

  • Revise

    Authors revise with an editor before the piece goes to faculty review.

  • Faculty sign-off

    Dr. Marsh gives final approval before the piece is typeset for the next issue.

Submit Your Paper

Ready to publish?

We accept submissions on a rolling basis from any DePaul student — you don't need to be a club officer or economics major. Papers should be 2,000–5,000 words with cited data.

Questions before submitting? Reach the editorial team at journal@depaul.edu.
