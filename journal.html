How It Works

How Publishing Works

  • Submit

    Send your piece through the submission form below, any time during the quarter — a course project, research, an event recap, an internship reflection, whatever connects back to economics.

  • Editorial support

    A student editor reads it over for clarity and makes sure your work is properly attributed to you.

  • Polish

    You and your editor make any final tweaks together before it goes to print.

  • Publish

    Your piece is typeset for the next issue, with your byline — ready to link on LinkedIn or add to your portfolio.

Submit Your Work

Ready to share what you've been working on?

We accept submissions on a rolling basis from any DePaul student — you don't need to be a club officer or economics major. Course projects, research papers, event recaps, internship reflections — if it connects back to economics, it belongs here. Our editors help you polish it and make sure it's properly credited to you, so it's ready to link on LinkedIn or add to your portfolio.

Questions before submitting? Reach the journal team at journal@depaul.edu.
