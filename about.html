About

Built by students who wanted economics off the page.

Our history, mission, vision, values, and the leadership philosophy that keeps the club running year after year.

History

From a study group to a journal

The Economics Club started in 2014 as a handful of students meeting weekly to discuss the Fed's post-recession policy moves. Within two years the group had outgrown a classroom, and by 2018 had published its first issue of the Economics Journal. Today the club runs a full journal, a speaker series, and six standing research initiatives.

What hasn't changed is the format: students proposing ideas, defending them in front of peers, and revising until the argument holds up.

Mission

Applied learning

Give DePaul students a place to apply economic reasoning to real, current problems — not just exam questions.

Vision

A known name

Become the first stop for DePaul students and employers looking for rigorous, student-led economic analysis.

Values

Rigor & access

Hold every argument to a research standard, while keeping the club open to any student who shows up curious.

Leadership

Teach, then step back

Officers mentor the next cohort deliberately, so the club's standards outlast any one board.

Leadership Philosophy

Officers lead. Members lead by second semester.

"We measure a good semester not by how many events we ran, but by how many members were ready to run one themselves." — Executive Board

Every officer role has a shadow track: incoming members pair with a current officer for a semester before taking on responsibility of their own. It's slower than just delegating tasks, but it's why the club has run without a gap for over a decade.
