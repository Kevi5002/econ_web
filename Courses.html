Recommended Electives by Career Track

Finance & Banking

ECO 315, ECO 322

Introduction to Money and Banking covers the Fed and financial markets; Financial Market Regulation builds on it with policy around banking, insurance, and financial services.

Public Policy

ECO 314, ECO 321

Economics of the Public Sector looks at government's role in fixing market failures; Law & Economics examines how business and government interact through regulation and antitrust.

ECO 307, ECO 308

Managerial Decision Making builds frameworks for better decisions under uncertainty; Business Strategy covers market selection, competitive dynamics, and resource allocation.

ECO 374, ECO 377, ECO 378

Health Economics looks at the supply and demand for medical care, the role of insurance, and how policy shapes health care costs — ties directly into the club's Healthcare Economics initiative.
